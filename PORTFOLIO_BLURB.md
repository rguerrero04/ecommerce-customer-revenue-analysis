# Portfolio copy

## Project card

**E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Analysis**

Analyzed 541,909 retail transaction lines with SQL and Pandas to identify retention opportunities, customer concentration risk, seasonal revenue patterns, and high-value RFM segments. Built a reproducible SQLite pipeline and executive-ready visuals with business recommendations.

## Resume bullets

- Analyzed 541K e-commerce transaction lines using SQL, Python, and Pandas; cleaned customer, order, and product data into a reproducible SQLite model covering £8.91M in valid sales revenue.
- Used cohort analysis, RFM segmentation, and SQL window functions to identify 655 at-risk customers with £1.15M in historical revenue and a 49-day median second-purchase window.
- Built an executive dashboard and translated revenue concentration, retention, market, product, and seasonality findings into measurable lifecycle and customer strategy recommendations.

## Interview explanation

“I wanted a project that showed more than technical syntax, so I started with a real business question: how an e-commerce retailer could increase revenue through retention. I cleaned over 500,000 transaction lines, modeled them into customer and order tables, and used SQL for cohort, RFM, and revenue-concentration analysis. The most important finding was that the top 10% of customers generated 61% of revenue, while 655 high-value customers had become at risk. I recommended a segmented win-back test and a first-to-second-order campaign timed before the observed 49-day median reorder point. I also separated an extreme one-order product outlier so it would not distort the merchandising recommendation.”
